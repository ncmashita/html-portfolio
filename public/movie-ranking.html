Nikki's New List of Awesome Movies

My Top 3 Holiday Movies of All-Time

The Holiday

- A sweet and silly holiday romance that's sure to warm your spirits.
- An adorably charming cast that's absolutely loveable.
- Delivers a gentle, feel-good emotion lift that leaves you smiling.

Four Christmases

- The frantic energy of juggling four separate family gatherings in a single day.
- Highlights the deeply flawed, eccentric, and messy family dynamics.
- Rooted in the main characters' pessimistic view of marriage and family.
- Packed with invasive questions and non-stop uncomfortable social situations.

Christmas With The Kranks

- Captures the intense, aggressive pack-mentality of the neighbors to peer pressure others into displaying over-the-top decorations.
- Centers on Luther and Nora's attempt to skip Christmas and take a tropical cruise.
- A mad, last-minute dash to pull together a massive Christmas celebration in just a few hours.
